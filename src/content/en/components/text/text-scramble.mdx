<Collapsible title="Usage">

The text scrambles through random characters and reveals from left to right. The animation plays on mount and replays on hover.

```tsx
<TextScramble>Enter your text here</TextScramble>
```

### Choose the wrapper element

Pass `render` to choose the wrapper element. Your props are merged with the component's, so it stays the right semantic tag while keeping the animation. Without `render` the text renders inside a `<span>`.

```tsx
<TextScramble render={<p className="text-4xl" />}>Enter your text here</TextScramble>
```

<Collapsible title="API" defaultOpen>

| Name | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `children` | `string` | — | Text content to scramble. Required. |
| `duration` | `number` | `0.7` | Total duration of the reveal in seconds. |
| `scrambleFps` | `number` | `30` | Number of scramble updates per second. |
| `playOnMount` | `boolean` | `true` | Plays the scramble animation on mount. |
| `playOnHover` | `boolean` | `true` | Replays the scramble animation on hover. |
| `characters` | `string` | `"abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz@!#*$%^&+_[]"` | Characters used to scramble. |
| `render` | `ReactElement \| function` | `<span/>` | Override the wrapper element. |

</Collapsible>
